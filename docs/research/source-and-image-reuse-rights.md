# Source and image reuse rights

Researched 2026-09-16. This is product-risk research, not legal advice. Rights must be checked per item and per source.

## Governing primary text

The WIPO Lex copy of Israel's [Copyright Act, 2007](https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/en/text/495410) records the enacted law and amendments available there. Relevant provisions include:

- **Sections 4–5:** original literary and artistic works can be protected; copyright does not extend to facts, data, ideas, or news of the day, but can protect their expression and an original compilation's selection or arrangement.
- **Section 6:** statutes, regulations, Knesset protocols, and judicial decisions are not protected by copyright.
- **Sections 11–16:** copyright includes reproduction, making available, and derivative works; electronic storage is reproduction and translation is a derivative work.
- **Section 19:** fair use can include research, criticism, review, journalistic reporting, and quotation, assessed by purpose, character, amount, substantiality, and market effect.
- **Sections 36 and 42:** other state-created works are not automatically public domain; state ownership and a 50-year term may apply.
- **Sections 45–46:** authors retain moral rights, including suitable attribution and protection against prejudicial distortion.

Therefore, “official,” “publicly visible,” or “about a candidate” does not by itself grant reuse rights.

## Default reuse matrix

| Material | Safe first-release default | Upgrade only when |
|---|---|---|
| Facts and data | Store normalized facts with provenance | Dataset-specific terms impose conditions or the selected arrangement is copied |
| Statutes, regulations, Knesset protocols, judgments | Quote or reproduce relevant portions with attribution and source link | The item contains separately protected attachments, images, or editorial material |
| Government or CEC web prose | Link and summarize independently | Explicit terms or written permission authorize copying |
| Candidate photographs | Use a placeholder | A license from the rights holder, CC license, or verified public-domain status is recorded |
| Journalism | Link, store bibliographic metadata, and use only a short necessary quotation after review | A license permits fuller reproduction |
| Books | Store title, author, publisher, edition, date, identifier, and link | Permission covers cover art, pages, or extracts; fair use is approved for a specific quotation |
| Party/list material | Link and summarize as a dated first-party statement | Published terms or written permission authorize copying media or text |
| Social posts | Store URL, author, platform, date, and reviewed short quotation where justified | Platform terms and the rights holder permit embedding, screenshots, or archival copies |

## Licensed and open material

Creative Commons' official [license overview](https://creativecommons.org/share-your-work/cclicenses/) states, among other distinctions, that CC BY requires attribution, CC BY-SA adds share-alike, ND licenses prohibit adaptations, and NC licenses permit only noncommercial use. For every reused item record the creator, source URL, exact license/version, retrieval date, required credit, permitted modifications, and evidence that the uploader controlled the rights. Cropping a photograph or translating text may be an adaptation, so ND material needs special care.

## Product rules implied by the evidence

- Prefer metadata plus a link. Copy only what provides clear reader value and has a documented legal basis.
- Keep quotations short and necessary; record the editorial fair-use rationale and never substitute a long extract for the original market.
- Do not treat attribution as permission.
- Do not assume Knesset, CEC, ministry, party, press, or social-media photographs are free to reuse.
- Preserve link-rot evidence as URL, title, publisher, publication/retrieval dates, and checksum. Keep full archival copies restricted unless permission or a counsel-approved exception applies.
- Use product-created neutral summaries rather than translating protected third-party prose.
- Provide a rights-owner correction/takedown channel and retain the audit event when public material must be removed.

## Unresolved legal questions

Qualified Israeli counsel should approve:

1. the quotation policy and per-item fair-use review threshold;
2. whether any thumbnail use is defensible without permission;
3. private preservation of removed or changed pages for evidentiary purposes;
4. embedding versus linking for each social platform;
5. whether the operator's funding makes NC material incompatible; and
6. the CEC and other government sites' current item-specific reuse terms.
