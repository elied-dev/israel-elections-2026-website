# Domain glossary

## Election Guide
The part of the product used to compare all officially registered electoral lists, their candidates, and their policy positions for a specific election.

## Election Edition
A dated, preserved Election Guide for one election. The first edition covers the 2026 Knesset election; support for later elections is a separate decision.

## Public Record
The part of the product used to find reviewed public sources associated with political actors and their past conduct.

## Political Actor
A registered candidate, elected official, electoral list, or political party represented by the product. The Public Record covers conduct relevant to the actor's public or electoral role, not unrelated private conduct.

## Electoral List
An officially registered list contesting an election.

## Candidate
A person appearing on an electoral list.

## Policy Position
A dated, neutral summary of a political actor's stated position, supported by quotations and links to original material.

## Contributor
An authenticated person who can submit a source proposal but cannot publish it.

## Source Proposal
A contributor's proposed article, book, or online source, including its links to political actors, descriptive tags, and a suggested relevance summary. Its review status and history remain visible even when it is rejected, subject to safety and privacy rules.

## Reviewer
An authorized person who approves or rejects source proposals and controls their published summaries.

## Review Decision
A reviewer's dated acceptance or rejection of a source proposal, accompanied by a public reason.

## Public Review History
The visible record of a source proposal and its review decisions. Harmful, illegal, or private material can be redacted while the fact of moderation and its reason remain visible.

## Source Record
A source proposal approved for inclusion in the Public Record. It contains source metadata, links to political actors, tags, and a reviewer-approved relevance summary.

## Informational Content
Site-authored material such as actor profiles, policy-position summaries, relevance summaries, tags, and review decisions. It must be available in Hebrew and English before publication.

## Source Language
The language in which an external source was originally published. The original source remains available regardless of whether translations exist.

## Translation Status
The visible state of a translation: reviewed, pending, or unavailable. The interface supports Hebrew, English, and French; French informational content can follow publication.
