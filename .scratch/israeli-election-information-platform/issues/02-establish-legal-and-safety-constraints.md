# Establish legal and safety constraints

Type: research
Status: resolved
Blocked by: none

## Question

Which Israeli laws, election-period rules, privacy obligations, defamation risks, accessibility requirements, and platform duties materially constrain a neutral election guide and a moderated public history of accepted and rejected source proposals? Identify requirements needing legal advice rather than treating research as legal advice.

## Answer

Keep the product informational: do not collect voting intentions, target users by political views, solicit votes, endorse lists, or run unreviewed political promotion. Contributor accounts require privacy, retention, access-control, security, and incident rules. Public review history must permit redaction of harmful or private material. Claims about past conduct need attribution, corrections, rights of reply, and legal escalation. Accessibility under Israeli Standard 5568 is a launch requirement. Israeli counsel must validate election-law classification, disclosures, Amendment 13 duties, defamation/redaction rules, and accessibility applicability before launch.
