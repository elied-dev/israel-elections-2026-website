# Establish reuse rights for sources and images

Type: research
Status: resolved
Blocked by: none

## Question

What may the product lawfully store, quote, thumbnail, or link from official records, journalism, books, social platforms, party materials, and candidate photographs? Identify practical licensing paths, attribution requirements, quotation limits, link-rot preservation options, and material that should remain link-only.

## Answer

Default to metadata, independent summaries, and links. Facts are not protected, but expression, compilations, photographs, translations, and electronic copies can be. Statutes, regulations, Knesset protocols, and judicial decisions have a statutory exception; other government works are not automatically public domain. Use short reviewed quotations only where justified, placeholders for unlicensed photos, and restricted rather than public archival copies. Record per-item rights and attribution; obtain counsel approval for fair-use, thumbnails, archiving, and platform embeds.
