# Map: Specify the Israeli Election Information Platform

Label: wayfinder:map

## Destination

Produce an agreed product specification, ready for implementation planning, for a trustworthy Hebrew-, English-, and French-language guide to the 2026 Knesset election and its linked public-source record.

## Notes

- Consult [`CONTEXT.md`](../../CONTEXT.md) for canonical domain language.
- Grilling tickets use the `grilling` and `domain-modeling` skills. Prototype tickets use the `prototype` skill. Research tickets use the `research` skill and prioritize primary sources.
- The product has linked Election Guide and Public Record views.
- Cover every officially registered electoral list and candidate without popularity filtering.
- Policy Positions combine neutral summaries with quotations and original sources.
- Do not publish ideological scores, truth ratings, voting recommendations, or unreviewed contributions.
- Contributors propose sources; authorized reviewers decide publication. Accepted and rejected proposals have a public history, with safety and privacy redactions where necessary.
- Informational Content requires reviewed Hebrew and English. The interface also supports French; French content may follow with a visible translation status.
- A small trusted editorial team operates the first release.
- Preserve the 2026 Election Edition as a dated archive.

## Not yet specified

- The sustainable ownership, funding, and volunteer model after the initial trusted team has operated the site long enough to expose the real workload.
- The user-feedback and product-measurement approach, pending concrete visitor journeys and launch criteria.
- Whether later election editions justify reusable automation, pending experience with the 2026 edition.

## Out of scope

- Voting advice, ideological scoring, truth scores, and endorsements.
- A general encyclopedia of all people associated with Israeli politics.
- Unrelated private conduct or personal information about political actors and their families.
- Direct or automatic publication of contributor submissions.
- Requiring translations of third-party source documents.
- Building future election editions as part of the 2026 specification.
